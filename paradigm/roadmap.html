Editorial Roadmap

Paradigm is being built in phases, deliberately. This page is an honest account of what's live today, what's actively being built, and what's planned for future publication cycles — updated as the platform develops, not written once and forgotten.

✓ Currently Available

✓
Research Library
Constitutional, position, discussion and framework papers — attributed, dated, versioned.
✓
Policy Watch
Official DfE, Ofsted, ICO and Education NI guidance, each linked to its source with Arena commentary.
✓
Observatory
Live tracking of official announcements — currently curated manually, reviewed by Arena rather than machine-scraped.
✓
Emerging Questions
Open, unresolved questions the sector hasn't settled yet, each grounded in current evidence.
✓
Knowledge Map
Topic relationships across guidance, papers and questions, built from Arena's live topic taxonomy.
✓
Evidence Trail
Follows a topic from official guidance through Arena's papers, discussion and further reading.
✓
Explore a Question
Guided topic search connecting official sources, commentary, papers and open questions.
✓
Conversations
Arena-curated discussion themes, each opened with a substantive starting post.
✓
Editorial Principles & Trust Centre
Editorial Standards, Publication Methodology, and how the whole platform is governed.

◐ In Development

◐
Forum — Open Submissions
Structured practitioner submission is built but inert — pre-moderation review will open once the underlying research base is strong enough to support genuine discussion.
◐
Automated Observatory Monitoring
Moving from manual curation to automated detection of new official publications.
◐
Full Cross-Document Search
Explore a Question already searches by topic; deeper full-text search across every paper and guidance document is being built.
◐
Research Library — Ongoing Expansion
The Library is live with a growing set of papers; new constitutional, position and discussion papers are added on an ongoing publication cycle.

○ Planned for the Next Publication Cycle

○
Practitioner Contributions
Open, reviewed submissions from practitioners outside Arena — evidenced case studies, not just opinion.
○
Threaded Community Discussion
Structured, moderated reply threads once the Forum opens beyond its current curated-topic phase.
○
Version Comparison
Side-by-side diff view when a paper or policy commentary is revised, beyond the version number shown today.
○
Deeper Topic Relationship Browsing
Extending the Knowledge Map beyond direct links into broader thematic clustering across the whole Library.
ⓘ This roadmap reflects the platform's actual current state and is updated as things ship — not aspirational marketing copy. See Why Paradigm Exists for the reasoning behind this approach.
